Greibach’s Hardest Context-Free Language

Tobias Nipkow 📧

August 25, 2026

Abstract

Formalization of Greibach’s hardest context-free language theorem: There is a “hardest” context-free language $L_0$ such that every context-free language is an inverse homomorphic image of $L_0$.
