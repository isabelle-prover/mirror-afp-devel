The Fundamental Group of the Circle

Arthur Freitas Ramos 📧, David Barros Hulak 📧 and Ruy Jose Guerra Barretto de Queiroz 📧

July 2, 2026

Abstract

We formalise the classical theorem of algebraic topology that the fundamental group of the circle is isomorphic to the additive group of the integers, $\pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$. The circle is modelled as the unit sphere in the complex plane with basepoint $1$, and the carrier of the fundamental group is the set of path-homotopy classes of loops based at $1$, with concatenation as the group operation. The group laws are obtained from the homotopy groupoid laws of the Isabelle Analysis library. The isomorphism with $\mathbb{Z}$ is given by the degree map, sending a homotopy class to the winding number about the origin of any representative loop. That this is a bijective group homomorphism follows from the winding-number classification of loops in the punctured plane, together with a radial retraction onto the circle. The entry is self-contained on top of the Isabelle distribution.
