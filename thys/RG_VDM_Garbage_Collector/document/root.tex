\title{Rely-Guarantee Specification of a Concurrent Garbage Collector}
\author{Roger C. Su and Nisansala P. Yatapanage}
\maketitle

%---------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{abstract}
We specify a model of the Ben-Ari concurrent garbage collector in the
rely-guarantee paradigm, and verify its correctness. The aim is to show
that the algorithm can be verified with rely-guarantee without the use of 
shared auxiliary variables. The approach is to reason that a certain
property is maintained by one component for all instances of a variable,
and then instantiate it to the particular instance required by the rely
of the other component.   
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Jones and Yatapanage \cite{Jones-Yatapanage-19} showed that Ben-Ari's concurrent garbage collector \cite{BenAri-84} is difficult to specify in the rely-guarantee paradigm without shared auxiliary variables, which break compositionality.
%
This proof-development, which accompanies \cite{Yatapanage-26a,Yatapanage-26}, expands on an alternative approach briefly mentioned in 
\cite{Jones-Yatapanage-19}. 
%
The idea is to maintain compositionality by using \emph{Holds For All} reasoning. The Mutator is shown to satisfy a certain property over all instances of a variable, without requiring any knowledge about which particular instance the Collector requires for its rely condition.
The property is then instantiated to the required context. 

The specification focusses on the Collector's Marking phase, which is where the interference from the Mutator manifests.
It specifies and establishes (1) that the Mutator maintains the property required for the Collector's rely to hold, and (2) that the postcondition of the Collector's Marking phase is reached.

Further details can be found in \cite{Yatapanage-26a,Yatapanage-26}.
