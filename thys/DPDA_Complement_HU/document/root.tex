\title{Deterministic Context-Free Languages are Closed Under
  Complementation (Hopcroft and Ullman)}
\author{Kaan Taskin and Tobias Nipkow}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
A deterministic context-free language (DCFL) is a language accepted by a deterministic pushdown automaton (DPDA).
This entry proves that the deterministic context-free languages are closed under complementation.
The proof follows the one in the book by Hopcroft and Ullman (1979) \cite{HopcroftU79}.
\end{abstract}
