\title{A deep embedding of HOL in HOL:\\ soundness, completeness, consistency}
\author{Christoph Benzm\"uller \and Daniel Kirchner}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
This entry presents a minimal, machine-checked deep embedding of
classical higher-order logic (HOL) in Isabelle/HOL, following
Benzm\"uller, Brown and Kohlhase (BKK)~\cite{BBK04}.  Throughout, HOL
means Church's simple theory of types~\cite{Church40}: it is the
embedded \emph{object} logic, while Isabelle/HOL serves as the ambient
meta-logic.  The language of BKK~\S2 is formalised in a \emph{locally
nameless} representation, which makes BKK's identification of
$\alpha$-equivalent terms literally true; no $\alpha$-conversion or
bound-variable renaming is ever needed.  The semantics of BKK~\S3 is
rendered abstractly, up to the class
$\mathcal{M}_{\beta\mathfrak{f}\mathfrak{b}}$ of $\Sigma$-Henkin
models; standard models arise as the full special case.

On this basis the natural-deduction calculus $\mathrm{NK}$ of BKK~\S7 is
proved sound (Theorem~7.3) and Henkin-complete, via the model-existence
method of BKK~\S6, with the term model realised as a term evaluation.
Completeness holds in a form stronger than BKK's Corollary~7.7: at
every infinite value carrier, for every signature with infinitely many
parameters, and for open formulas.  It also holds relative to
hypotheses.  For derivability from hypotheses in Andrews' sense---some
finite part of the hypothesis set (the \emph{context}) derives the
conclusion---any context of
open formulas is admitted, at any signature cardinality (any parameter
type of the meta-logic), once the
carrier is at least as large as the signature; semantic compactness of
the Henkin consequence follows.  For the calculus itself, a finite
context needs nothing beyond well-formedness, and a pure context of
open formulas is admitted over a countable signature at every infinite
carrier.  Consistency---that
$\bot$ is not derivable---follows from a concrete $\Sigma$-standard
model with finite domains: a single individual and finitely many
non-logical constants suffice, so no axiom of infinity is assumed for
the embedded logic.  Model existence is likewise concrete: from the
consistency of a sentence over a countable signature, a countable
Henkin (general) model of it is defined outright in plain
HOL---non-effectively, via a maximal-consistent extension, but with
consistency as the only premise.  The signature includes BKK's optional
primitive equality (Remark~7.9) and---following
Andrews~\cite{Andrews72}, beyond BKK---typed description operators.  As
an illustration, Cantor's theorem is derived \emph{inside} $\mathrm{NK}$,
in surjective and injective form and at every
type~\cite{SEPChurchTT}.  Finally, by compactness over arbitrarily
large finite models, $\mathrm{NK}$ is shown consistent with a schematic
diagram forcing infinitely many individuals---no infinite model is
constructed (Andrews' route, \cite{Andrews02}~\S55)---and the single
Dedekind-style axiom of infinity is shown not to follow from that
scheme: no finite part of the scheme derives it, and a countable Henkin
model of the whole scheme refutes it.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\paragraph{What is new.}
Concurrently and independently, From, Huerta y Munive and
Schlichtkrull~\cite{Q0CompletenessAFP} mechanised completeness of
Andrews' $\mathcal{Q}_0$ for general models; their development builds on
D\'iaz's \emph{Metatheory of $\mathcal{Q}_0$}~\cite{Q0MetatheoryAFP} and
thereby on set theory axiomatised in HOL (\texttt{ZFC\_in\_HOL}).  The
present entry instead embeds the object logic and constructs its models
in plain Isabelle/HOL, with no set-theoretic universe --- which is what
we mean by HOL \emph{in} HOL.  To the best of our knowledge it is the
first machine-checked completeness proof for a deep embedding of HOL over
plain HOL; Harrison's self-verification of HOL Light~\cite{Harrison06}
concerns soundness and consistency, not completeness, and the nearest
completeness results treat weaker logics (second-order logic in
Coq~\cite{KochKirst22}; a synthetic framework for propositional,
first-order, modal and hybrid logic~\cite{From25}).  The entry formalises
BKK's model-existence method (BKK~\S6) --- the saturated
maximal-consistent extension and its closure ($\nabla$) properties ---
for the Henkin class $\mathcal{M}_{\beta\mathfrak{f}\mathfrak{b}}$, and
\emph{extends} BKK by typed description operators after
Andrews~\cite{Andrews72} (the rule $NK(\iota)$ and its model condition,
carried through soundness, model existence and completeness).
Completeness is obtained in a form stronger than BKK's Corollary~7.7:
for open formulas, at every infinite carrier, and for every signature
with infinitely many parameters, with no cardinality link between the
two.  It also holds in the hypothesis-relative form
$\Phi \models A \Rightarrow \Phi \vdash A$, carried all the way to
arbitrary pure contexts of open formulas.  A transfinite extension
lemma takes this beyond countable signatures, to BKK's arbitrary
signature cardinalities $\aleph_s$ (Remark~3.16, ``arbitrary'' meaning
arbitrary within the type universes of the meta-logic, instantiation by
instantiation); here the carrier must be at least as large as the
signature, and purity scales to the signature.  Throughout, the trade
of free variables for fresh parameters is made simultaneously, by a
substitution-value law for the whole context at once.  For derivability from hypotheses in
Andrews' sense (some finite sub-context derives the conclusion) every
purity proviso disappears, and semantic compactness of the Henkin
consequence is obtained as a corollary, with no ultraproducts.

\paragraph{Consistency: two tiers.}
Two further points concern \emph{consistency} rather than completeness.
As a by-product, the finite-model consistency argument is pushed to the
infinity scheme: by compactness, applied to arbitrarily large \emph{finite}
models, $\mathrm{NK}$ is shown consistent with a scheme forcing
infinitely many individuals --- entirely within plain HOL, with no
infinite model constructed and no strengthening of the meta-theory by a
set-theoretic universe.  The complementary direction concerns
$\mathrm{NK}$ with a \emph{single} Dedekind-style axiom of infinity.
Such an axiom admits no finite models and so falls outside the plain-HOL
setting of this entry; it is handled by constructing a standard model
over an axiomatic set-theoretic universe such as Paulson's
\texttt{ZFC\_in\_HOL}~\cite{ZFCinHOL}---the price being a stronger,
non-conservative meta-theory, and hence only \emph{relative} (to
$\mathrm{ZF}$) consistency.  That model has full function spaces, so it
also validates the relational axiom of choice.  The same route therefore
yields the relative consistency of full classical higher-order logic
($\mathrm{NK}$ with the infinity axiom and choice, extensionality and typed
description being already present).  Writing \emph{M-HOL} for the
meta-logic Isabelle/HOL and \emph{O-HOL} for the embedded object logic,
this entry establishes $\text{M-HOL}\vdash\mathrm{con}(\text{O-HOL})$
\emph{outright}---that is, relative only to plain HOL itself: no set
universe and no strengthening of the meta-theory certify the object
logic consistent---no conflict with G\"odel's second theorem, since
O-HOL, having finite models, lacks the arithmetic strength the theorem
presupposes.  The single-axiom direction above is the other
tier: with the infinity axiom and choice O-HOL is itself Church's simple type theory
with infinity and choice, which interprets the M-HOL core and so shares its
proof-theoretic strength---a \emph{metatheoretic} observation, not a
machine-checked one (for the strength of the Zermelo-style set theories in
play see~\cite{Mathias01}; the definitional apparatus of the M-HOL core is
separately known consistent~\cite{KuncarPopescu19}).  Plain M-HOL therefore
cannot certify it without proving its own consistency, which G\"odel's second
theorem bars, so a set universe $V$ becomes necessary, giving
$\text{M-HOL}+V\vdash\mathrm{con}(\text{O-HOL}+\text{infinity}+\text{choice})$;
metatheoretically this is consistency relative to $\mathrm{ZF}$, in the
sense the companion's introduction makes precise.  That tier---with an abstract universe
interface, abstracted further to a Harrison-style axiom, and infinity as
the only surplus (choice comes for free)---is carried out in a
companion development~\cite{InfinityZFC}, submitted to the Archive as
\texttt{HOL\_in\_HOL\_Deep\_Infinity\_ZFC}.  Neither tier is
the object logic proving its own consistency (cf.\ Harrison's
self-verification of HOL Light~\cite{Harrison06}); always M-HOL, one level up.

\paragraph{Compatibility with the published version.}
This update is additive.  The headline interface of the August 2026
version is preserved: theory names, the exported theorems of
\texttt{Main\_Results} with their statements, and the Cantor results
under their published names, \texttt{cantor\_surjective} and
\texttt{cantor\_injective}, kept as compatibility aliases; further
published lemmas that the generalised proofs no longer need retain their
published statements and are marked as compatibility exports in place.  All new
material---the strengthened completeness ladder, the finitary
consequence relation, theory \texttt{NK\_Infinity} with the
scheme/axiom separation---is added on top.  Two deliberate exceptions,
flagged for maintainers: the theorem \texttt{completeness} now takes the
weaker hypothesis-relative premise (BKK's Corollary~7.7 proper; a caller
holding a validity proof adapts by one step), and a handful of internal
auxiliaries superseded by the generalised proofs were retired---both
documented in the change log of this update.
