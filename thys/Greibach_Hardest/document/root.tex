\title{Greibach's Hardest Context-Free Language}
\author{Tobias Nipkow}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Formalization of Greibach's \cite{Greibach73} \emph{hardest context-free language theorem}:
There is a ``hardest'' context-free language $L_0$ such that every context-free language
is an inverse homomorphic image of $L_0$.
\end{abstract}
