\begin{isamarkuptext}%
Substep counter / phase indicator for the output machine's
  state set, mirroring AE's \isa{substep{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}idx}.  The five
  simulation sub-tags \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimRead} /
  \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimCompute} / \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimWrite} /
  \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimAdvance} / \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimNext} mark the five
  phases of one M-step simulation; \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}HaltAccept} and
  \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}HaltReject} are the AR-specific terminal states
  that map to the substrate's \isa{t{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}tm\ M{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}} and
  \isa{r{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}tm\ M{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}}.  No validation sub-tags: AR's language
  theorem is quantified over encoder-image inputs only, and
  AR's per-tape \isa{buf} field is populated on-the-fly
  during read substeps (no buffer-initialisation prelude to
  motivate a validation phase).
  Throughout, \isa{b} abbreviates \isa{block{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}width\ {\isasymGamma}} (the per-symbol cell width).%

\begin{isamarkuptext}%
Per-tape position-kind tag, the AR analogue of AE's
  per-tape regime dispatch (\isa{le{\isadigit{0}}} / \isa{le{\isadigit{1}}} /
  \isa{steady}), generalised to three values because of the
  LE-1-cell / proper-b-cell layout: \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}AtLE} for the
  head at M-position 0 (the substrate-mandated single
  \isa{LE{\isadigit{4}}} cell), \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}AtFirstProper} for the head at
  M-position 1 (immediately past the LE cell, where an
  \isa{L}-move lands on the LE cell), and
  \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}AtFurtherProper} for the head at M-position
  \isa{{\isasymge}} 2 (where an \isa{L}-move stays in proper
  territory).  Read by \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimRead} / \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimWrite}
  to dispatch between LE-mode and proper-mode handling, by
  \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimAdvance} to compute the per-direction
  displacement, and updated by \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimAdvance} per the
  direction.%

\begin{isamarkuptext}%
Stage bookkeeping carried in the output machine's state
  set: substep tag, current-tape index (\isa{nat}), per-bit
  counter (\isa{nat}), per-tape symbol buf
  (\isa{nat\ {\isasymRightarrow}\ {\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}a}: holds the decoded value after read, the
  symbol-to-encode during write), direction-vector
  (\isa{nat\ {\isasymRightarrow}\ dir}: emitted by \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimCompute},
  consumed by \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimAdvance}), and per-tape position-kind
  (\isa{nat\ {\isasymRightarrow}\ ar{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}pos{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}kind}: read by SimRead/SimWrite for
  LE/proper dispatch, updated by SimAdvance per the direction).
  The \isa{nat} counter is loose-typed; \isa{ar{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}valid{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}stage}
  below imposes the bound \isa{i\ {\isacharless}{\kern0pt}\ {\isadigit{2}}\ {\isacharasterisk}{\kern0pt}\ b}
  (accommodating the \isa{{\isadigit{2}}b}-cell L-walks of
  \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimAdvance}) to recover finiteness of the state
  set.%
\end{isamarkuptext}\isamarkuptrue%

\begin{isamarkuptext}%
State-level stage validity: the \isa{nat\ {\isasymRightarrow}\ {\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}a} buf
  field stores per-tape symbols in
  \isa{{\isasymGamma}\ {\isasymunion}\ {\isacharbraceleft}{\kern0pt}bl{\isacharbraceright}{\kern0pt}} (so \isa{finite\ {\isasymGamma}} bounds it),
  and the \isa{nat} bit-counter is bounded by
  \isa{{\isadigit{2}}\ {\isacharasterisk}{\kern0pt}\ b} (the factor \isa{{\isadigit{2}}}
  accommodates \isa{AR{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}SimAdvance}'s L-walks of up to
  \isa{{\isadigit{2}}b} cells; AR's \isa{{\isasymdelta}{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}} keeps the
  counter's substantively-reachable range within this bound,
  so the bound is non-restrictive on runtime states but
  load-bearing for finiteness of the state set).  The
  position-kind field is unconstrained at this level — its
  type is already finite.  Spec-side counterpart used as the
  stage-component restriction in \isa{alphabet{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}reduce}'s
  output state set \isa{Q{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}}, making \isa{Q{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}} finite from
  the set-level premise \isa{finite\ {\isasymGamma}} plus the
  value-level tape-count bound (\isa{ar{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}stage{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}bounded}, next block)
  — without requiring
  \isa{UNIV{\isacharparenleft}{\kern0pt}nat\ {\isasymRightarrow}\ {\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}a{\isacharparenright}{\kern0pt}} to be a finite type.  Direct AR
  analogue of \isa{ae{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}valid{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}stage}.%
\end{isamarkuptext}\isamarkuptrue%
