\begin{isamarkuptext}%
The output machine's \isa{{\isasymdelta}{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}} is defined as a
  union of five per-substep transition relations, mirroring
  AE's \isa{alphabet{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}enlarge{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}delta} shape.  Each substep
  relation is a set of substrate-shape 5-tuples
  \isa{{\isacharparenleft}{\kern0pt}{\isacharparenleft}{\kern0pt}q{\isacharcomma}{\kern0pt}\ stg{\isacharparenright}{\kern0pt}{\isacharcomma}{\kern0pt}\ a{\isacharcomma}{\kern0pt}\ {\isacharparenleft}{\kern0pt}q{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}{\isacharcomma}{\kern0pt}\ stg{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}{\isacharparenright}{\kern0pt}{\isacharcomma}{\kern0pt}\ a{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}{\isacharcomma}{\kern0pt}\ d{\isacharparenright}{\kern0pt}} where
  \isa{q} ranges over \isa{Q{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}tm\ M}, \isa{stg}
  over \isa{{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}a\ ar{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}stage}, \isa{a} /
  \isa{a{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}} over \isa{nat\ {\isasymRightarrow}\ sym{\isadigit{4}}}, and
  \isa{d} over \isa{nat\ {\isasymRightarrow}\ dir}.  The five substep
  relations are defined below, one per simulation phase.

  No validation cluster: AR's language theorem is quantified
  over encoder-image inputs only, so non-canonical sym4 inputs
  are outside the theorem's scope and M''s behaviour on them is
  unconstrained.

  The union is intersected with two global restrictions:
  LE-preservation (no transition forges \isa{LE{\isadigit{4}}} out of
  a non-\isa{LE{\isadigit{4}}} cell, matching the substrate's
  \isa{{\isasymdelta}LE} invariant) and
  \isa{ar{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}valid{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}stage}-membership on both source and
  target stages (matching the non-product \isa{Q{\isacharprime}{\kern0pt}} shape).
  Throughout, \isa{b} abbreviates \isa{block{\isacharunderscore}{\kern0pt}width\ {\isasymGamma}} (the per-symbol cell width).%
